Project Overview
Des-Lux Play is a luxury stuffed toy business founded by Des-J Sekgobela in 2020 in Burgersfort, Limpopo, South Africa. This website serves as the brand’s digital storefront, showcasing products, enabling custom order enquiries, and communicating the company’s charitable mission.
Website Goals and Objectives
•	Establish a professional online presence
•	Drive custom order enquiries and showcase limited-edition releases
•	Promote the monthly giveaway held every last Friday
•	Transparently communicate the 10% charity pledge
Key Features and Functionality
•	5 responsive HTML pages with semantic structure
•	Two-column layout (content + sidebar) on all pages
•	Custom order enquiry form with file upload
•	General contact form with message categorization
•	Two location maps (Burgersfort and Polokwane)
•	Charity impact highlight section
Sitemap
•	Homepage (index.html) — Hero banner, collections preview, charity highlight, giveaway
•	About Us (about.html) — Founder story, mission/vision, charity details
•	Products (products.html) — Standard, custom, and limited-edition collections
•	Enquiry (enquiry.html) — Custom order form with toy specifications
•	Contact (contact.html) — General contact form, two locations with maps
